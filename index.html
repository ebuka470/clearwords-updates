Meet your language partner

Not a tutor. A cousin.

Timmy doesn't say "Great job!" like a kindergarten teacher. He says "Ah, see you — that's clean."

He codeswitches the way real Nigerians do. He drops proverbs and then translates them, a little smug about knowing them. He tells you about the time he greeted his landlord wrong and his mum found out.

He corrects you gently. He never breaks character to explain what he is. And when you're nervous, he tells you about when he was worse than you.

Questions

The things people ask.

Is ClearWords really free?
Yes. 50 lessons per language, 200 Timmy messages a day, up to 3 pods, 1 language exchange partner, and progress cards — all free forever. Premium unlocks unlimited lessons, unlimited Timmy chats, and voice calls with your partner.
Who is Timmy, really?
Timmy is your language partner. He's the cousin who grew up abroad, moved back to Lagos, and learned his own language the hard way. He blends real Nigerian speech with grammar help, cultural context, and his own learning stories. He doesn't lecture. He chats.
I understand the language but can't speak it. Will this help?
That's exactly the gap ClearWords is built for. Most heritage learners understand way more than they can produce. Timmy draws that out with real conversation practice — no shaming, no pressure, just talking.
Which languages do you support?
Yorùbá, Igbo, Hausa, and Nigerian Pidgin are live. Urhobo and Itsekiri are in development.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Install ClearWords as an app (via your browser's "Add to Home Screen") and your lessons and progress work without internet. Timmy needs a connection, but your structured lessons don't.
How long until I can hold a conversation?
Most learners handle basic conversations within 6–8 weeks of 15 minutes a day. Full conversational comfort usually takes 6 months to a year. It depends on how much you practice. Timmy will keep you going.

The language is yours.

You don't need to be perfect. You just need to start. Timmy's waiting.
